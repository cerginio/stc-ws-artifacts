About the project

StoryTileCraft Community Edition product is oriented toward specialists working with trauma and parents who want to support kids consciously.

StoryTileCraft is the web platform with application and methodology materials ready for instant use like a first aid box for psychotherapy.

StoryTileCraft Community Edition is free and doesn’t contain any ads or registration. Methodology includes therapeutic scenarios for storytelling, basic guides on how to use stories, digital drawing and gamification tools.

Materials and tools are intended for psychologists, psychotherapists, social workers, and can also be used by non-specialists, such as parents.

Detailed about methodology, stories scenarios, learning materials, collaboration see on: storytilecraft.com

To open an encrypted story from another user or device, enter a guest passphrase.

The passphrase used for your own encrypted stories (set in the “Manage Passphrase” dialog) will not be replaced by the guest passphrase.

The guest passphrase applies only to this specific "Open Encrypted File" dialog and is not preserved after the application closes.
